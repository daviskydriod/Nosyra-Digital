

# Structural redesign update

The homepage and portfolio are being recomposed as an editorial digital-studio experience rather than a recolor: split hero composition, oversized type, project-led bento showcase, filterable portfolio archive, lead case-study card, and a separate social content gallery. Inspired by broad patterns observed in Envato agency portfolio templates and Dribbble agency/case-study references; no code or visual assets are copied. The public redesign intentionally avoids gradients in favor of flat navy surfaces, solid overlays, cyan rules, restrained borders, and image-led storytelling.
